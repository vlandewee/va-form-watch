# VA Form Watch Terms of Service

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

By subscribing to VA Form Watch, you agree to these terms. They are short, and
we wrote them in plain language.

## What the service does

VA Form Watch checks the VA's public VA Forms API once a day. If a VA form was
published, updated or removed, we email subscribers a list of those forms with
links.

## What the service is not

- **Not the VA.** VA Form Watch is run by an independent developer. It is not
  part of, or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.
- **Not legal advice.** Alerts do not tell you which form to use or how to fill
  it out. Always confirm the current form on [VA.gov](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/).
- **Not guaranteed.** We do our best, but alerts can be late, missing or wrong,
  for example if the VA's data is delayed. Do not rely on this service as your
  only way to track VA forms.

## Cost

The service is free. We do not sell your information or show ads.

## Your responsibilities

- Subscribe only with an email address you own or are allowed to use.
- Do not use the service for anything illegal.

## Ending the service

You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any alert. We may
change or stop the service at any time. If we stop it, we will email subscribers
and delete the subscriber list.

## Your privacy

Our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy) explains what we collect and how to have it
deleted.

## Limits on liability

The service is provided "as is," without warranties of any kind. To the extent
the law allows, we are not responsible for any loss caused by a late, missing or
incorrect alert.

## Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will email subscribers before the change takes
effect and update the date at the top.
