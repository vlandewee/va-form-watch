# VA Form Watch

VA Form Watch is a free email alert service. Once a day, it checks the VA's
public VA Forms API. If a VA form was published, updated or removed, it emails
subscribers a list of those forms with links to the official versions.

VA Form Watch is run by an independent developer. It is not part of, or
endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, and it is not legal advice.

## Subscribe

Email [va.form.watch@gmail.com](mailto:va.form.watch@gmail.com?subject=subscribe)
with the subject "subscribe." To unsubscribe, reply "unsubscribe" to any alert.
