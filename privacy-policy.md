# VA Form Watch Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

VA Form Watch is a free email alert service. It tells subscribers when a VA form
is published, updated or removed. It is run by one independent developer. It is
not part of, or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA).

This policy explains what information we keep, why, and how you can have it deleted.

## What we collect

We collect **only your email address**, and only when you ask to subscribe.

We do not collect your name, location, health or financial information, or any
VA records. We do not use tracking pixels, cookies or analytics in our emails.

The form information in our emails comes from the VA's public VA Forms API. It is
public information and contains nothing about you.

## How we use your email address

We use it for one purpose: to send you VA form change alerts. We may also send a
rare notice about changes to this service or this policy.

We do not use your email address for advertising or marketing.

## Who can see your email address

- **The developer** who runs the service, to add or remove you from the list.
- **Google (Gmail).** Our alerts are sent from a Gmail account, so Google
  processes your address to deliver the email. The developer also keeps a
  working copy of the subscriber list in the service's Google account, protected
  by two-step verification. It is deleted from there at the same time as
  everywhere else. Google's privacy policy applies to that processing.
- **GitHub.** The subscriber list is stored as an encrypted secret in GitHub,
  where the service runs. It is never stored in code or shown in logs.

Each alert is sent to you alone, so other subscribers never see your address.

We will **never sell, rent or trade your information** for money or anything
else. We do not share it with marketers or any other third party. Any third
party that handles your data for us must keep the same promises we make here.
No one may use or share your information, including in de-identified form,
without your consent.

## How long we keep it

We keep your email address only while you are subscribed. If the service sends
you nothing for a long time, we still keep your address until you unsubscribe or
the service shuts down.

## Unsubscribing and deleting your data

To unsubscribe, reply to any alert with the word "unsubscribe," or email
[va.form.watch@gmail.com](mailto:va.form.watch@gmail.com).

When you unsubscribe, we permanently delete 100% of your data within 7 days.
That is well within the VA's 45-day requirement. Since your email address is the
only thing we keep, nothing about you remains afterward.

Copies of past emails may remain in the sending account's "Sent" folder until
they are deleted. We delete those along with your address.

## Security

- Your email address is kept only in GitHub's encrypted secrets and the
  service's Google account. Our passwords are stored as encrypted secrets.
- The accounts that run the service use two-step verification.
- Alerts are sent over an encrypted connection.

## If there is a data breach

If we learn that your email address was exposed, we will email you within 7 days.
We will tell you what happened and what, if anything, you should do.

## If the service changes owners or shuts down

We will email all subscribers before any change of ownership. You can unsubscribe
and have your data deleted before the change. Any new owner must follow this
policy. If the service shuts down, we will delete the entire subscriber list.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will email subscribers before the change takes
effect and update the date at the top.

## Contact

Questions? Email [va.form.watch@gmail.com](mailto:va.form.watch@gmail.com).
